SkinRoutine Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the SkinRoutine iOS app (version 1.0 and later) handles your personal data. The app is developed by Şule Yılmaz.

In short

Unless you use the AI assistant or sign in with Apple, SkinRoutine does not collect any personal data; all your information stays on your device. If you sign in with Apple, your data is stored on our server so it can sync between your devices (see "Account and sync" below). If you use the AI assistant and Premium, the data described under "AI assistant and Premium" below is processed with your separate, revocable consent.

Information you enter

You can enter:

  • Your skin type and skincare goals
  • Your products, product photos, opening and expiry dates
  • Your morning and evening routines and the steps you complete
  • A skin diary: notes, observations and skin photos
  • Your reminder times

Unless you sign in with Apple, all of this is stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage, and we cannot access it. If you sign in, the "Account and sync" section below applies.

Skin photos and notes about your skin may be sensitive. For this reason the app does not transfer them off your device unless you sign in with Apple and use sync. Skin photos and diary notes are never sent to the AI assistant.

Backups

If iCloud Backup or computer backups are turned on for your iPhone, app data is included in Apple's device backup. These backups are subject to Apple's privacy terms and are not accessible to us.

From Profile → Backup you can export all your data (including photos) as a single file and save it wherever you choose (for example iCloud Drive). The file stays where you save it; it is not sent to us.

Product search by name

When you search for a product by name, only the search text you type is sent to Open Beauty Facts (openbeautyfacts.org), an open and free cosmetics database, and matching products' names, brands and ingredient lists are returned. Nothing about you (name, account, location, photos) is sent. Using this feature is optional.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for routine and product reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification server is used. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Camera: used only when you choose to take a photo. Photos stay on your device; the only exception is a package photo you take to scan an ingredient list with Premium (see the AI assistant section below).
  • Photos: only the photo you pick is imported; the rest of your library is not accessed. If you choose "Save Image" when sharing your routine card, the card is added to Photos; only add-only permission is requested.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

SkinRoutine does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

AI assistant and Premium

The first time you open the AI assistant, a separate permission screen is shown. If you agree:

  • What is sent: your question; your skin type and goals; ingredients you want to avoid; the names of your routine steps; your products' names, brands, categories, ingredient lists and (if you did one) patch test result.
  • Ingredient list scanning (Premium): When you use this feature, the package photo you choose or take is passed through our server to Anthropic to read the ingredient list. The photo is processed only for that request and is not stored on our server. Please use only photos of product packaging.
  • What is never sent: your skin photos, your profile photo, your skin diary and your notes.
  • Identity: No account is required. The app creates a random, anonymous user ID for you. It contains no email, name or phone number.
  • Where it's processed: Data is processed on our Supabase servers in the European Union (Ireland); to generate answers, the question and context are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, outside Türkiye. Your chat history, consent records and daily usage counts are stored on our server.
  • Premium: Purchases are made through Apple; we never see your payment details. To check that a subscription is valid, the Apple-signed transaction ID and expiry date are stored on our server.
  • Withdrawing consent: You can withdraw your permission at any time in Profile → Withdraw AI data permission.
  • Deletion: Your AI data on our server is permanently deleted with Profile → Delete all my data. If you have already removed the app, email sulesyy@icloud.com.

AI answers are not medical advice.

Account and sync

Sign in with Apple is optional. If you sign in, your profile (skin type, goals, ingredients you avoid, reminder settings), products, routines, routine completion records, skin diary and the photos attached to them are stored on our server (Supabase, EU – Ireland) so they appear on every device where you sign in with the same account. This data may include information about your skin condition. It is used only to provide the service, is never shared, and is not used for advertising or analytics. Access is limited to your account; photos are kept in private storage.

When you sign in with Apple, we ask Apple only for your name, never your email address. The name Apple shares (or the name you enter on your profile) is stored with your account so it can be shown on your profile and restored when you sign in on another device. Signing out removes the name from the device; deleting your account removes it from our server too. If you choose a profile photo, it stays on your device only and is never uploaded.

We do not ask Apple for your name or email address; your account is identified by an anonymous identifier provided by Apple. When you sign out, your data stays on your device and syncing stops. Use the option below to delete server data.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your data at any time from Profile → Delete all my data. This permanently deletes the data on your device and, if you used the AI assistant or signed in with Apple, your account (anonymous or Sign in with Apple), all synced records and photos, AI chat history, consent records and usage counts on our server. If you signed in with Apple, the app's access to your Apple ID is also revoked. Deleting the app alone does not remove server data; use this option first. Your App Store subscription is managed by Apple; cancel it in iPhone Settings → Subscriptions.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children; it does not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If future versions add an account, cloud backup or similar features, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested before those features are used.
